Who We Are

Dedicated to safeguarding the marine biodiversity of Algoa Bay and Nelson Mandela Bay.

Our History

Founded in 2018 in Gqeberha, the Trust was established by local marine biologists to protect Algoa Bay's coastal ecosystems and endangered African Penguin populations. We directly combat pressing environmental challenges across the Nelson Mandela Bay region—from marine plastic pollution and habitat loss to coastal urban runoff.

Mission Statement

To protect Gqeberha's marine life by empowering local schools, youth groups, and Eastern Cape communities with practical environmental knowledge. We conduct interactive workshops, marine awareness campaigns, guided educational sessions, and active coastal cleanup initiatives to drive real conservation action.

Vision Statement

To establish Algoa Bay as a world-leading, sustainably managed marine sanctuary. We foster robust local and international research partnerships, ensuring that every conservation strategy implemented is driven by empirical data, technological innovation, and long-term ecosystem sustainability.
